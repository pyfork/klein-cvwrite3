Before you start: your privacy

Better Jobs writes a CV for a job ad you paste. To do that, we need some of your data. Here's a summary of the notice.

  • What we collect: your email, your master CV, your contact details, the ads you paste, the CVs we write, payment references and any problem reports.
  • Why: to write and check your CVs and your interview prep, keep track of your credits and payments, and answer your requests.
  • Who receives it: providers that host our server, send our emails, protect the site, run the AI and store backups. Some are outside Malaysia.
  • Where it's kept: on a secured server.
  • How long: runs and PDFs for 30 days, or 3 months if you choose. Your profile until you delete it. Backups for up to 14 days. Payment records as long as accounting needs, with no CV content.
  • Your choices: download, correct or delete your data at any time on the Account page. For anything else, email privacy@puascari.com. We reply within 21 days.
  • Do you have to? Giving your data is voluntary, but we can't write a CV without it.
