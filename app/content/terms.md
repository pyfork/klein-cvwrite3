## In short
You pay only for CVs that pass their check, credits last a year, and unused paid credits can be refunded.

## 1. Credits
- One credit is one CV that passes its check: 2 pages, A4, with the ad's top keywords that your master CV backs up.
- If a CV doesn't pass, we don't use a credit.
- Each CV comes with 3 free re-renders after you edit it. After that, a new CV costs 1 credit.
- New accounts get 2 free credits. If you deleted an account in the last 12 months, a new account doesn't get them again.

## 2. Prices and payment
- 5 credits for RM10, or 20 credits for RM30.
- You pay by DuitNow QR, with the order reference in the transfer note.
- We add credits once your payment is confirmed, so none are added before the money has arrived.

## 3. Expiry
Credits expire 12 months after we add them, the free ones included. We always use your oldest credits first. Your balance shows the next expiry date.

## 4. Refunds
1. Free credits can't be refunded.
2. Unused paid credits that haven't expired can be refunded at the price you paid: RM2 each from a RM10 pack, RM1.50 each from a RM30 pack.
3. Expired credits can't be refunded.
4. Refunds only go back to the bank account that paid.
5. You can always delete your account. If you have money pending or unused paid credits, we'll refund what's owed.
6. If Better Jobs closes, we'll refund every unused paid credit without being asked.

## 5. Fair use
Up to 20 CVs and re-renders a day per person. Don't upload other people's CVs, or anything you don't have the right to use.

## 6. Your CV
You're responsible for checking that your CV is true before you send it. We can't promise interviews or jobs.

## 7. When the service is paused
If we pause generating to fix a problem, your saved requests and credits are safe. If a pause lasts more than 3 days, we may extend your credits' expiry by the length of the pause.

Email privacy@puascari.com, or use "Report a problem" at the bottom of any page.
