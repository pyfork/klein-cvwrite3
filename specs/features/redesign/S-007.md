# Slice S-007: When a signed-out visitor opens `/`, they see the Better Jobs home.

Feature: [brief](brief.md)
Slice: 7 of redesign

## Outcome
When a signed-out visitor opens `/`, they see the Better Jobs home: the accepted collage homepage v2 (Addendum 18's target), with the art from slice 6 and only the invented samples. Today `/` sends them to the sign-in form. A signed-in visitor is redirected as now. Brief Addendum 15: "`/` shows the home to a signed-out visitor"; the Shape's risky slice 7 (ACCESS): `/` can show a public page without weakening any other redirect or exposing account data, and the soft sign-in check does not start a session.

## Rules (the Lead's: routes, access, headers, files)

AC-95 Given a visitor with no session cookie, or one whose session has ended, expired or does not exist,
      when they open `/`,
      then they get 200 and the home page, not a redirect to `/signin`. The answer sets no cookie, and its `Cache-Control` is `private, no-store` (Addendum 6), because `/` answers differently by sign-in. Opening it writes nothing to the database: no session row is made, changed or slid.

AC-96 Given a signed-in visitor,
      when they open `/`,
      then they are redirected exactly as today: 303 to `/request` if they are not on the list, to `/consent` if the current notice needs their consent, to `/profile` until the profile is ready, and to `/generate` once it is. Their session slides as it does today.

AC-97 Given a signed-out visitor,
      when they open any page other than `/`,
      then they get exactly what they get today: every page that sends a signed-out visitor to `/signin` (303) today still does, and every page open to them today (sign-in, the notice, the terms, `/deleted`, `/static/`, `/fonts/`) still is. Only `/` changes.

AC-98 Given the home page,
      when its HTML is read,
      then every image, stylesheet, font and script it loads is served by the app (`/static/` or `/fonts/`, never another host), the stylesheet and art URLs carry `?v=<app version>`, and every script it loads is on the allow-list (slice 4's test) for the home's template. No personal data: no email, name, balance or account detail of anyone appears; people, employers and numbers are the three invented sample roles, labelled as made up.

AC-99 Given the home page with scripts off, or with reduced motion,
      when it loads,
      then every section, word and link is in the HTML and works, and each motion shows its end state (Addendum 15: "the hero shows its end state").

Screen rules (layout, type, colour, motion, the six responsive checks, light and dark) are the designer's, from AC-100 on.

## Out of scope
- **Any other page:** every other page keeps its markup (slices 8 to 13).
- **A personal part on the home** (a "welcome back" for a signed-in visitor): safe because a signed-in visitor never sees the home; revisit if the home ever gains one (the Shape's key choice: then pin "the same for every visitor").
- **Public caching of `/`:** safe because `/` stays `private, no-store`; revisit if the home moves to its own URL.
- **Malay copy:** the feature brief's out of scope.

## Touch points (planned; the build PR measures)
- `app/routes_cv.py` (ACCESS on the risk map): the `/` handler reads the session without requiring it; signed out gets the home, signed in goes through the existing checks.
- `app/templates/index.html`: new, the home.
- `app/static/betterjobs.css` (SHARED on the risk map): the home's sections, if the shell's rules do not cover them.
- One or more small scripts in `app/static/`, each on the allow-list (`tests/test_scripts_allowlist.py`, SHARED): the hero's play-once switch.
- `tests/test_signin.py`: the three cases that pin `/` → `/signin` for a signed-out or expired session now expect the home (AC-95 says so).
- `app/main.py` (SHARED): `APP_VERSION` bump for the new `?v=` key, unless the move to `app/version.py` lands first (the plan's Owed).
- `CHANGELOG.md`: one line.
