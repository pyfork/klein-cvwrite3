## Rules (moved from S-008, word for word)

#### See it work (navy band)

AC-118 Given the "See it work." band,
       when it renders,
       then it is navy (`--band`; dark `#1A2C49`), full width, 64 px from its neighbours, with torn top and bottom edges drawn by the `edge.webp` mask (1200 by 45 px, repeated across; the bottom one flipped and shifted 410 px); its head holds the heading, the lede and the scissors art tilted −8°, and the art is not shown below 700 px (decoration only).

AC-122 Given the panel at 961 px or wider,
       when it renders,
       then it has two columns (`minmax(0,.92fr) minmax(0,1.08fr)`): the clipping and the reading at the left, the meter and the slider at the right; the slider is 700 px tall.
       Below 961 it is one column with the meter and the slider first and 560 px tall, because the before and after is the point of the section; the clipping and reading follow. The slider block holds the only controls in the panel, so moving it up moves no control past another (S-001 AC-26 holds).

AC-123 Given the clipping,
       when it renders,
       then it is the ad as torn paper in its sample's colour with a soft drop shadow: the company (a label), the ad's title, its meta line, a rule, the problem quotes, each on a cream highlight with its number in coral-for-text, and "+ N more lines in the ad".
       On teal paper the words are cream on a darker teal, `#1B7369` (5.03:1), only where words sit; the swatches and the art keep teal (approved 2026-11-04: ledger row D2) [v2: cream on `#26968A`, 3.2:1].

AC-124 Given the reading under the clipping,
       when it renders,
       then it holds "What we found in the ad" with each problem numbered (ink number on a cream disc), its plain-words name and its quote in italic, then "Top 3 keywords" as three outlined chips, each with its label "strong match" or "good match".

AC-125 Given the meter above the slider,
       when it renders,
       then it reads "Top 3 keywords: X of 3 before, Y of 3 after, from word Z", with the before count in `--on-band-2` and the after count in coral (5.12:1 on navy), counted from that sample's two CVs.

AC-131 Given scripts off,
       when the band renders,
       then the tab list is not shown, the three samples' panels show one after another, each headed by its tab's words, and each slider shows its "after" sheet whole with no grip, tag or "Drag" label (Addendum 15: the slider shows "After"); every word of all three samples is in the HTML.

AC-132 Given the band,
       when it ends,
       then the italic note says the people, companies and numbers are made up and what the highlighted words are (Addendum 14's label).

#### The keywords

AC-133 Given the keywords slab,
       when it renders,
       then under the heading and lede a summary line reads the before count in photocopy ink and the after count in coral-for-text ("Before: 3 of 9 keywords." / "After: 9 of 9, all in the first 50 words."), counted from the samples on the page.

AC-134 Given each of the three people at 901 px or wider (three columns) or below (one column, the two pages 420 px wide at most),
       when their block renders,
       then it holds their swatch, name and ad title, a "Before" page and an "After" page side by side, each with a dashed marigold band at the top labelled "First 50 words", and one sticker per top-3 keyword at the place it first appears (photocopy stickers before, coral after; a sticker never covers another); under "Before" the missing keywords are named, or "All 3 there."; under "After", "N of 3 in the first 50 words."; then a "See {name}'s CV" link.

AC-135 Given the "See {name}'s CV" link,
       when it is pressed,
       then that sample's tab is selected and the page scrolls to the band (smoothly, at once with reduced motion); with scripts off it is a plain link to that sample's panel.

AC-136 Given the stickers,
       when the page renders,
       then their places are in the HTML, so they show with scripts off; with scripts on, no reduced motion, and the grid starting below the first screen, the "after" stickers rise once into place when 45% of the grid is in view (60 px, 1.4 s, each 0.35 s after the last, starting 0.4 s after), and otherwise they simply sit there.
