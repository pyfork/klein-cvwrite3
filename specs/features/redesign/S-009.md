# Slice S-009: The home's frame, header and hero exist behind the flag `CVWRITE_HOME`, so that slice 13 can show them at `/`.

Feature: [brief](brief.md)
Slice: 9 of redesign

## Outcome
With the setting `CVWRITE_HOME` on (dev and tests only: production refuses it, S-008 AC-185), a visitor with no session cookie who opens `/` sees the home's frame, its header and its hero, as they show with scripts off, in place of slice 8's placeholder. With the setting off, `/` answers exactly as today (S-008 AC-184). Slice 10 adds "See it work." and the keywords below the hero, slice 11 the bottom half, slice 12 the demonstrations, and slice 13 shows the home at `/`.

Why a prerequisite slice: the home is built behind the flag (Addendum 22), so no visitor sees this slice until slice 13. The top half as one slice was estimated at about 1,130 changed lines with the samples' words, over the 700-line stop (the plan review of #143, finding S7); the owner split it on 2026-11-08 at the place v2 itself splits: the hero is fixed markup, while "See it work." and the keywords are drawn from the three samples' words.
