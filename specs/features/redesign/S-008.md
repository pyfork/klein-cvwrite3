# Slice S-008: The flag `CVWRITE_HOME` exists, so that slices 9 to 11 can build the home behind it and slice 12 can show it at `/`.

Feature: [brief](brief.md)
Slice: 8 of redesign

## Outcome
With the setting `CVWRITE_HOME` on (dev and tests only: production refuses it), a visitor whose request carries no session cookie who opens `/` gets 200 and the home page, `index.html`; in this slice that page is a placeholder that says the new home is being built. Slices 9 to 11 build the real page behind the setting, and slice 12 removes it. With the setting off, the default and the only value production takes, `/` answers exactly as today.

Why a prerequisite slice: the home as one slice was estimated at about 1,500 changed lines of product code and 83 rules (the feature brief, "Changed in the plan for slice 8"). The owner split it on 2026-11-07 (Addendum 22). The plan review of the first slice 8 plan (#143, finding S3) then found the flag and its access path bundled with the top half; a risky slice holds nothing else, so the flag is its own slice, as slice 5 was split out of #124. The home is five slices: 8 the flag, 9 the top half, 10 the bottom half, 11 the demonstrations, 12 the home goes public (the ACCESS change).

## Which slice proves each rule
Slice 8 proves the Lead's flag rules AC-184 to AC-186. The screen rules AC-100 to AC-177 and AC-95 to AC-99 are held below for slices 9 to 12.

## Rules (the Lead's: the flag)

AC-184 Given the setting `CVWRITE_HOME` unset or `off` (the default),
       when anyone opens `/`,
       then they get exactly what they get today: a visitor with no session, or one whose session has ended, expired or does not exist, gets 303 to `/signin`; a signed-in visitor gets today's redirects (`/request`, `/consent`, `/profile` or `/generate`). This holds until slice 12 removes the flag (a flag-off case, retired then by a re-plan of this check).

AC-185 Given `CVWRITE_ENV=prod`,
       when the settings load with `CVWRITE_HOME=on`, or with any value other than `on` or `off`,
       then loading fails with a `ConfigError` that names `CVWRITE_HOME`, so production cannot show the half-built home; `off` and unset load as before. In dev, `on` and `off` load and any other value fails.

AC-186 Given `CVWRITE_HOME=on` (dev),
       when a visitor whose request carries no session cookie opens `/`,
       then they get 200 and the home page (`index.html`: in slice 8 a placeholder that extends `base.html` and says the new home is being built; slice 9 replaces it with the top half), with `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and no cookie set; a request that carries a session cookie, live or not, goes through today's checks unchanged (an ended or expired one still gets 303 to `/signin` until slice 12 adds the soft sign-in check).
