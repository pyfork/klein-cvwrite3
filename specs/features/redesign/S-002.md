# Slice S-002: When anyone opens a page of the app, the light look matches the target design where it can be read.

Feature: [brief](brief.md)
Slice: 2 of redesign

## Outcome
On every page, in light, the brand mark is coral as in the target; muted text and the focus ring take the target's colours on white slabs, and keep the darker, readable ones on the ground and the quiet slab.

## Rules
This slice adds no new AC. It builds slice 1's rules as re-planned on 2026-10-29 (S-001):

- **AC-9** muted text: `#6A7586` on white slabs (the target); `#566275` on the ground and the quiet slab, where `#6A7586` is under 4.5:1.
- **AC-27** brand mark: coral, as in the target.
- **AC-44** focus ring: coral where a white slab is behind it (3.09:1); ink on the ground, on the quiet slab and on a coral button, where coral is under 3:1 or vanishes.

AC-16 and AC-17 (4.5:1 text, 3:1 rings) still hold everywhere; slice 1's CHECK 2 measures them.

## Out of scope
- **Dark mode:** slice 3.
- **Any other colour, size or layout:** unchanged from slice 1.
- **Every page's own markup:** the old class names stay.

## Touch points
- `app/static/betterjobs.css`: a second muted token, the brand mark's fill, the focus-ring colour by surface.
- `CHANGELOG.md`: one line.
