## 1. Who we are
Better Jobs is run by KIP ADVISORS (Registration No. 201803365653, Company No. 002900789-V), registered in Malaysia ("we"). To reach us about your data, email privacy@puascari.com.

## 2. What we collect
All of it comes from you, as you use Better Jobs.
- If you join the waitlist: your email, the note you write, and whether you want one email when there's room.
- Your email address, from sign-in. To stop misuse of sign-in, we also keep a scrambled form of your internet address for a day.
- Your master CV and the text we read from it.
- Your contact details and standing edits.
- The job ads you paste, and the CVs we write from them.
- Payment references and amounts. We never collect card details or payer names.
- Problem reports you send us, and the reason you pick if you mark a CV "not good".

## 3. Why we use it
To write and check your CVs and your interview prep, keep track of your credits and payments, answer your requests, and keep the service working and safe. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Sensitive data
A CV can mention race, religion or health. We only use these to write your CV and your interview prep, and only if you agree on a separate line. Please don't include your IC number: if we spot one, we'll ask you to remove it before anything is sent to the AI.

## 5. Who receives it
We use a few providers to run Better Jobs. Their contracts with us require them to keep your data secure and use it only to provide their service to us.

| Provider type | What it does | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting and email | Runs our server and sends our emails | Your account data and sign-in codes; your email, for one email when there's room if you asked for it, and a thank-you when your payment is confirmed |
| AI | Reads the ad and writes the CV | The ad and your CV, without your name or contact details. It doesn't train on them and deletes them after a limited period |
| Security | Protects the site and carries your visits to our server | Your visits |
| Backups | Stores our backups | Encrypted backups only |

Our providers may process your data outside Malaysia, under their contracts with us.

## 6. Where it's kept, and how it's protected
On a secured server. Admin access is restricted and logged. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server. Our logs never hold your CV, ads or contact details.

## 7. How long we keep it
- CVs, ads, runs and PDFs: 30 days. If you tick "Keep my applications for 3 months", 3 months. You can change this at any time.
- Your profile: until you delete it.
- Sign-in codes: a day. Signed-in sessions: 30 days after you last use Better Jobs, or until you sign out.
- Closed problem reports: 12 months.
- The waitlist: until you join Better Jobs, 30 days after we email you, or 12 months, whichever is first. A declined request: 12 months.
- Technical logs, with a scrambled account id and none of your CV, ads or contact details: 90 days. Error records: 12 months.
- Backups: up to 14 days.
- Orders and credit records: as long as accounting needs, with no CV content.
- Your consent record, after you delete your account: 12 months. It's proof that you agreed, and it stops repeat free credits.

## 8. Your rights
You can see, download, correct or delete your data at any time on the Account page. Deleting your account also withdraws your consent. For any other request, email privacy@puascari.com. We reply within 21 days.

## 9. Do you have to give your data?
No, it's voluntary. But we can't write a CV without it.
