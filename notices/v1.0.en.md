## 1. Who we are
cvwrite3 is run by [data user's name] ("we"). To reach us about your data, email [request email].

## 2. What we collect
- Your email address, from sign-in. To stop misuse of sign-in, we also keep a scrambled form of your internet address for a day.
- Your master CV and the text we read from it.
- Your contact details and standing edits.
- The job ads you paste, and the CVs we write from them.
- Payment references and amounts. We never collect card details or payer names.
- Problem reports you send us.

## 3. Why we use it
To write and check your CVs, keep track of your credits and payments, answer your requests, and keep the service working and safe. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Sensitive data
A CV can mention race, religion or health. We only use these to write your CV, and only if you agree on a separate line. Please don't include your IC number: if we spot one, we'll ask you to remove it before anything is sent to the AI.

## 5. Who receives it
- **Anthropic** runs the AI that reads the ad and writes the CV. The ad and your CV, without your name or contact details, are sent to it outside Malaysia. [Anthropic's API retention and training terms, to be confirmed.]
- **[email provider]** sends your sign-in codes. It receives only your email address and the code, [where the provider processes email].
- **Cloudflare** protects the site and passes your visits on to our server.
- **DigitalOcean** stores encrypted backups in Singapore.

## 6. Where it's kept, and how it's protected
On our server in [server country]. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server. Our logs never hold your CV, ads or contact details.

## 7. How long we keep it
- CVs, ads, runs and PDFs: 30 days.
- Your profile: until you delete it.
- Sign-in codes: a day. Signed-in sessions: 30 days after you last use cvwrite3, or until you sign out.
- Closed problem reports: 12 months.
- Backups: up to 14 days.
- Orders and credit records: as long as accounting needs, with no CV content.
- Your consent record, after you delete your account: 12 months. It's proof that you agreed, and it stops repeat free credits.

## 8. Your rights
You can see, download, correct or delete your data at any time on the Account page. Deleting your account also withdraws your consent. For any other request, email [request email]. We reply within 21 days.

## 9. Do you have to give your data?
No, it's voluntary. But we can't write a CV without it.
